Formerly Center for Guidance Services (CGS)

The Center for Guidance and Counseling Services (CGCS) is an integral part of the university which commits to develop and deliver comprehensive guidance programs that will holistically empower students to make informed educational, social, and personal-career choices towards becoming productive, globally competitive, and emotionally resilient citizens.

Our Vision

A premier student-centered guidance and counseling office committed to nurturing psychological well-being, emotional resilience, and lifelong personal excellence for the Heron community.

Our Mission

To develop proactive, accessible, and evidence-based guidance and tele-counseling interventions that foster holistic growth and student academic triumph under Republic Act No. 9258.

Core Functions and Guidance Pillars

1
Personal, Social, Spiritual & Career Development

Facilitates and helps students in the achievement of their personal, social, spiritual and academic development as well as the acquisition of relevant knowledge and skills in career planning.

2
Holistic Growth & Responsive Counseling

Promotes holistic development and helps students to become well-adjusted individuals through responsive guidance and counseling services in line with the year level thrust and the university's vision-mission.

3
Preventive & Proactive Guidance Programs

Creates preventive/proactive guidance programs that lead to the enrichment and satisfaction of students learning and experiences necessary for a successful and well-balanced student life.

University of Makati CGCS Privacy Policy

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) & RA 9258 (Guidance and Counseling Act)

1. Purpose Specification & Clinical Scope

The Center for Guidance and Counseling Services (CGCS) collects and processes personal information exclusively for bona fide students and personnel of the University of Makati. All academic information, contact details, standardized psychometric assessments (PHQ-9 and GAD-7), and medical/medication disclosures are gathered strictly to facilitate clinical triage, confidential counseling, and emergency intervention programs.

2. Technical Safeguards & Cryptographic Storage

Sensitive health history (physical illness, current and past maintenance medications) and counseling SOAP notes are encrypted at rest using military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption. Encryption keys are securely managed server-side. Decrypted clinical records are accessible solely to assigned licensed Registered Guidance Counselors (RGC) and the CGCS Directorate on a strict need-to-know basis.

3. Data Retention & Secure Disposal Boundaries

In accordance with clinical standards set by the Professional Regulatory Board of Guidance and Counseling and CHED institutional guidelines, counseling case folders and psychometric records are maintained securely for a maximum period of five (5) academic years following student graduation or separation from UMak. Upon expiry of the retention window, digital clinical files are permanently wiped and anonymized.

4. Student Data Subject Rights (Section 16, RA 10173)

As a data subject, you retain full statutory rights under Philippine law:

  • Right to be Informed: To know how your clinical data is gathered, evaluated, and protected.
  • Right to Access: To review personal intake records and counseling session schedules.
  • Right to Rectification: To request correction of outdated or inaccurate personal and academic details.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request suspension of data processing, subject to mandatory university clinical safety protocols.
  • Right to File a Complaint: Before the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if your privacy rights have been violated.
5. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

For any inquiries regarding data protection or to exercise your statutory rights, please reach out to the official University of Makati Data Protection Office at dpo@umak.edu.ph or the CGCS Directorate at cgcs@umak.edu.ph.

CGCS Terms of Service & Clinical Protocol

Institutional Guidance Policies, Tele-Counseling Code of Conduct & Emergency Protocols

1. Non-Monetary Institutional Student Welfare Policy

All counseling, psychological screenings, crisis triage, and mental health interventions provided by the University of Makati Center for Guidance and Counseling Services (CGCS) are 100% tuition-funded institutional welfare services. Under no circumstances are monetary fees, commercial transactions, billing, or charges assessed for any counseling service. No monetary refunds or fees apply.

2. Tele-Counseling Code of Conduct

Virtual counseling sessions are conducted in a secure, confidential environment. Both the student and counselor must ensure attendance from a private, quiet physical room free from external disruptions. Mutual respect, professional boundaries, and honest communication must be observed at all times.

3. Strict Prohibition on Audio/Video Recording and Screen Captures

ABSOLUTE CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-RECORDING CLAUSE: To ensure the complete psychological safety of students and clinical integrity of licensed counselors, audio recording, video recording, live streaming, photographing, or screen-capturing tele-counseling sessions is STRICTLY PROHIBITED.

Any unauthorized recording or distribution constitutes a grave violation of the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), the Anti-Wiretapping Act (RA 4200), and the UMak Student Code of Discipline, and will result in immediate termination of the session, referral to the Student Disciplinary Board, and appropriate legal action.

4. Limits of Confidentiality & Emergency Intervention

All guidance communications are strictly confidential, except where Philippine law and clinical ethics mandate immediate protective intervention:

  • When there is clear, imminent danger of self-harm or suicide.
  • When there is clear, imminent danger of severe harm to others.
  • Mandated reporting of child or elder abuse, or compliance with a valid court order.

In such emergencies, counselors are required to initiate university crisis protocols, alerting the UMak Medical Clinic, student emergency contacts, and the National Center for Mental Health (NCMH 1553).

5. In-Person Desk & University Physical Office

Walk-in triage and in-person consultations are available at the CGCS Office, Admin Building Room 304, University of Makati, open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Emergency walk-in crisis triage does not require prior appointment booking.
